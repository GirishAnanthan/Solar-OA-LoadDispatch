Time-of-Day (TOD) Tariff Arbitrage Strategy

Under SERC industrial tariffs, electricity drawn during the evening peak window (18:00 – 22:00) incurs a 20% to 30% surcharge (up to ₹10.50 – ₹12.00/kWh). When BTM Rooftop Solar generation exceeds factory demand around midday (11:00 – 14:00), the Reverse Power Relay would otherwise curtail solar to enforce Zero-Export. The BESS intercepts this curtailed energy at zero marginal cost and discharges it during the evening peak, maximizing financial yield.
